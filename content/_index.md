Welcome to Serge! Serge is a storm chasing team based out in Wisconsin, aiming to collect data from severe thunderstorms with custom made probes. Working with the National Weather Service, the end goal is for this data to be used to better predict forecast models. Making each day a little safer, one chase at a time!
